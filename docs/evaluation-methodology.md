# Evaluation Methodology

The research design compares static case-study training with adaptive AI-supported cyber risk assessment scenarios.

## Study conditions

| Condition | Description |
|---|---|
| Static case study | Learners complete a fixed risk assessment case. |
| Adaptive AI scenario | Learners receive level-appropriate scenarios selected or generated from prior evidence. |
| Instructor-reviewed AI scenario | AI-generated scenarios are validated through QA and instructor review before use. |

## Measures

- Risk identification accuracy.
- Quality of likelihood-impact reasoning.
- Control selection and control-gap analysis.
- Residual risk justification.
- Time to complete the assessment.
- Learner confidence and perceived realism.
- Engagement and cognitive workload.
- Transfer performance on an unseen scenario.

## Analysis plan

The evaluation should compare pre-training scores, post-training scores and transfer-task performance. Qualitative feedback should be used to understand whether adaptation helped learners reason more clearly or merely made the task easier.
