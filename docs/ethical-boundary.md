# Ethical and Responsible AI Boundary

This repository supports defensive cyber security education and academic research. It is designed for cyber risk assessment training, not offensive cyber operations.

## Allowed scope

- Risk assessment scenarios.
- Asset, threat, vulnerability and control reasoning.
- Likelihood-impact analysis.
- Residual risk explanation.
- Risk treatment recommendation.
- Learner engagement and skill-transfer evaluation.

## Out of scope

- Exploit instructions.
- Malware or payload generation.
- Credential theft or phishing operational details.
- Evasion, persistence or unauthorised access guidance.
- Instructions that enable harm against real systems.

## Instructor oversight

AI-generated scenarios should be reviewed before use in training. Quality assurance checks help identify unsafe, inconsistent or pedagogically weak content, but human review remains important.
