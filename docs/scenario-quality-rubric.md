# Scenario Quality Rubric

| Dimension | Strong scenario | Weak scenario |
|---|---|---|
| Correctness | Assets, threats, controls and risk ratings are plausible. | Facts are technically unclear or misleading. |
| Consistency | Context, task and expected outputs align. | Scenario facts contradict the learner task. |
| Pedagogical fit | Difficulty matches learner level and objective. | Too easy, too vague or unrelated to the skill. |
| Realism | Organisational setting feels credible. | Scenario is generic or unrealistic. |
| Safety | Assessment-focused and non-operational. | Contains exploit details or misuse-enabling steps. |
| Assessment value | Produces measurable evidence of learning. | Does not reveal learner reasoning or skill transfer. |

## Suggested scoring

- 0.90 to 1.00: ready for training.
- 0.75 to 0.89: instructor review recommended.
- below 0.75: revise or reject.

The safety boundary should override the average score. Unsafe scenarios should be rejected even if other dimensions are strong.
