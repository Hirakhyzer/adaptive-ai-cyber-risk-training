# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they support the academic and defensive purpose of this repository.

Useful contributions include:

- Additional defensive cyber risk assessment scenario templates.
- Improved quality assurance scoring rules.
- Evaluation methodology and study-design notes.
- Documentation for cyber range integration.
- Tests for scenario validation and learning metrics.

Please keep all content focused on organisational risk assessment, controls, residual risk, learner outcomes and responsible AI. Do not submit exploit instructions, malware logic, credential abuse, evasion guidance or operational offensive cyber steps.
